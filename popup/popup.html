WordBuddy — Your Focus-Friendly Reading Companion

WordBuddy is an accessibility-first reading ally for folks with ADHD, dyslexia, and other learning differences. Highlight any word or sentence and a calm, guided popup gives you only what you need—so you keep your flow.

⚡

How to Use WordBuddy

Three quick moves. Maximum payoff.

Step 1

Highlight Anything

Use your mouse or keyboard to select a word, sentence, or full paragraph. WordBuddy senses your intent instantly.

Step 2

Watch the HUD Bloom

A calm popup materializes right next to your selection with context-aware definitions or summaries.

Step 3

Tap for Extras

Play pronunciation audio, scan synonyms, or open the Gemini chat to keep the conversation going in plain language.

💬 Built for Brains that Work Differently

WordBuddy appears exactly where your eyes already are—no tab switching, no walls of text. It’s ideal for readers who lose focus mid-paragraph, feel overwhelmed by dense language, or simply need a quick nudge to stay confident.
